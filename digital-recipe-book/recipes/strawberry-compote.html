  • Adjust the amount of sugar and lemon juice to your liking! Add more sugar for a sweeter compote or a little extra lemon juice for a tangier flavour.

Helpful tips!

1/2 tbsp lemon juice 

1/3 cup white sugar

2-3 cups sliced strawberries

Ingredients

Firstly, wash and slice your strawberries into small pieces. Add them to a saucepan along with the sugar and lemon juice, stirring until everything is evenly combined.



​

​

Once it begins boiling, reduce the heat to low and allow the mixture to simmer for 20–30 minutes, stirring constantly. As it cooks, the strawberries will break down and the sauce will gradually thicken to a jam-like consistency.

Next, place the saucepan over medium heat and bring the mixture to a gentle boil, stirring occasionally as the strawberries begin to soften and release their juices.



​

​

Once your compote has reached your desired consistency, remove the saucepan from the heat and allow it to cool slightly. Transfer the compote to a clean, airtight container and refrigerate until completely chilled before serving.
