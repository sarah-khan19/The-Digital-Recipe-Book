Banana Bread

This banana bread is soft, moist, and full of sweet banana flavour. A true family favourite for breakfast or dessert!

Makes one 9 x 5-inch loaf
Prep Time 20 mins
Cook Time 60 mins

Ingredients

  • 3-4 ripe bananas
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup softened unsalted butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts (optional)

Helpful tips!

  • The butter may look slightly separated after adding the eggs. Don't worry! This is completely normal, and your banana bread will bake just fine.

Instructions

1

Firstly, preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease a 9 × 5-inch loaf pan with butter, making sure to coat the bottom and sides to prevent sticking.

2

Next, in a large mixing bowl, cream together the butter and sugar until the mixture forms a smooth paste.

3

Mash the bananas in a separate bowl until smooth with small lumps remaining.

4

Once mashed, add the bananas and eggs to the butter and sugar mixture. Mix until combined.

5

Stir in the vanilla extract.

6

In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, mixing until just combined.

7

If using, fold in the chopped walnuts.

8

Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan and smooth the top.

9

Bake for approximately 60 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the centre comes out clean. Let cool in the pan for 10 minutes before transferring to a wire rack.
