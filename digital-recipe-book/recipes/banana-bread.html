  • The butter may look slightly separated after adding the eggs. Don't worry! This is completely normal, and your banana bread will bake just fine.

Helpful tips!

Firstly, preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease a 9 × 5-inch loaf pan with butter, making sure to coat the bottom and sides to prevent sticking.



​

​

Then, mash the bananas with a fork in a separate bowl until they reach a smooth, baby-food-like consistency. Add the vanilla extract and mix until combined.

Next, in a large mixing bowl, cream together the butter and sugar until the mixture forms a smooth paste.



​

​

Once mashed, add the bananas and eggs to the butter and sugar mixture. Mix until combined.

Meanwhile, in a small bowl, whisk together the flour, salt, and baking soda.

Gradually, add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, stirring until just combined. Be careful not to over mix, as this can make the banana bread dense.

If desired, sprinkle chopped walnuts, other nuts, or chocolate chips over the top for some extra flavour and texture.

Finally, pour the batter into your prepared loaf pan and bake for approximately 1 hour.

Once baked, insert a toothpick into the centre of the loaf. It should come out with a few moist crumbs, but no wet batter. Allow the banana bread to cool for at least 30 minutes before slicing and serving.
