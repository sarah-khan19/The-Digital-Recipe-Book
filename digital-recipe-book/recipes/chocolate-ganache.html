Chocolate Ganache

Decadently rich and irresistibly silky. Perfect for frosting, filling, or drizzling over your favourite desserts!

Makes about 3/4 cup of ganache
Prep Time 5 mins
Cook Time 10 mins

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup semisweet chocolate
  • 1/2 cup heavy whipping cream

Helpful tips!

  • Adjust the chocolate-to-cream ratio to achieve your desired consistency! Use more chocolate for a thicker ganache perfect for spreading, or more cream for a thinner ganache ideal for drizzling.

Instructions

1

Firstly, measure out your bittersweet chocolate, using either chocolate chips or a bar chopped into small pieces. Place the chocolate in a heat-safe bowl.

2

Next, pour the heavy whipping cream into a small saucepan and heat over medium-low heat until small bubbles begin to form around the edges. Avoid bringing the cream to a full boil.

3

Pour the hot cream over the chocolate and let it sit for 1–2 minutes without stirring, allowing the chocolate to soften.

4

Gently stir until the mixture becomes smooth, glossy, and fully combined. Let cool slightly before using as a drizzle, or chill for a thicker, spreadable consistency.
