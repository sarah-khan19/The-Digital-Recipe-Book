1/2 cup heavy whipping cream

1/2 cup semisweet chocolate

Ingredients

Firstly, measure out your bittersweet chocolate, using either chocolate chips or a bar chopped into small pieces. Place the chocolate in a heat-safe bowl.

​

​

Carefully pour the warm cream over the chopped chocolate and let it sit for 1–2 minutes to soften the chocolate. Then, gently stir until the chocolate has completely melted and the ganache becomes smooth, glossy, and well combined.

Next, pour the heavy whipping cream into a small saucepan and heat over medium-low heat until small bubbles begin to form around the edges. Avoid bringing the cream to a full boil.



​

​

Lastly, allow the ganache to cool slightly before serving or using it in your favourite desserts. It will thicken as it cools, making it perfect for drizzling, spreading, or dipping!
