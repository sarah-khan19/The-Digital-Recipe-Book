Lemon Poppyseed Loaf

This loaf is soft, moist, and delightfully tangy with a bright lemon flavour and a subtle crunch from poppyseeds.

Makes one 9 x 5-inch loaf
Prep Time 25 mins
Cook Time 50 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • rind of 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp poppyseeds

Helpful tips!

  • Mix the juice of one lemon with ¼ cup of powdered sugar to create a smooth, zesty glaze to drizzle over your loaf!

Instructions

1

Firstly, preheat your oven to 350°F and grease a 9 × 5-inch loaf pan with butter to prevent sticking.

2

Next, grate the rind of one lemon to create fresh lemon zest. Set aside for later.

3

In a medium bowl, cream the butter and sugar together until light and fluffy.

4

Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition.

5

In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt. Alternate adding the dry ingredients and milk to the butter mixture, mixing until just combined.

6

Fold in the lemon zest and poppyseeds until evenly distributed throughout the batter.

7

Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan and bake for approximately 50 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the centre comes out clean. Cool before slicing.
