Helpful tips!

Firstly, preheat your oven to 350°F and grease a 9 × 5-inch loaf pan with butter to prevent sticking.



​

​

In a large mixing bowl, combine the melted unsalted butter and white sugar, stirring until well incorporated.

Next, grate the rind of one lemon to create fresh lemon zest. Set aside for later.



​

​

Gradually, add the eggs, milk, flour, baking powder, and salt, mixing after each addition until the batter is smooth and everything is well incorporated.

After mixing thoroughly, fold in the lemon zest and poppy seeds. Feel free to adjust the amount of poppy seeds to your liking.

Finally, pour the batter into your prepared loaf pan and bake for approximately 50 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the centre comes with a few moist crumbs.

Once baked, allow the loaf to cool for at least 30 minutes before removing it from the pan and slicing. Enjoy!
