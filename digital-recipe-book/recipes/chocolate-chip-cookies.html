Firstly, in a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt until evenly combined. Set aside.



​

​

Then, add the eggs to the butter and sugar mixture, whisking until just combined. Be careful to not over mix.

Next, in a separate large bowl, use an electric mixer to cream the softened butter with the brown sugar and white sugar until the mixture becomes light, fluffy, and pale brown.



​

​

Gradually, add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, folding gently until a dough forms. Use your hands if needed to bring the dough together.

Once combined, fold in the chocolate chips, adding as many as you like! Feel free to customize your cookies with other mix-ins, such as chopped nuts, M&Ms, or white chocolate chips.

Next, chill the dough in the refrigerator for at least 20 minutes. Meanwhile, preheat your oven to 350°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

Once chilled, scoop the dough into medium-sized balls and place them onto the prepared baking sheet, leaving enough space between each cookie to allow for spreading. Bake for approximately 15 minutes

Finally, let the cookies rest on the baking sheet for 20 minutes after removing them from the oven. They may look slightly underbaked at first, but don't worry! They'll continue to cook and set as they rest.
