Chocolate Brownies

These brownies are gooey, fudgy, and packed with rich chocolate flavour. Perfect for satisfying any sweet craving!

Makes one 8 x 8-inch pan of brownies
Prep Time 15 mins
Cook Time 25 mins

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup sunflower oil
  • 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup self-rising flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Helpful tips!

  • These brownies taste even better the next day! Bake them the night before and store in an airtight container once cooled.

Instructions

1

Preheat your oven to 360°F. Lightly grease an 8 × 8-inch brownie pan with sunflower oil, then line it with parchment paper.

2

Add the chocolate chips and sunflower oil to a pot. Heat over low heat, stirring constantly until the chocolate has just melted and the mixture is smooth. Be careful not to use high heat to melt the chocolate, as it can burn easily.

3

In a medium mixing bowl, lightly beat the eggs and vanilla extract until combined. Be careful to avoid over mixing. Pour in the melted chocolate mixture and stir vigorously until everything is fully incorporated.

4

Sift the flour into the batter in batches, mixing between each addition until fully combined. This helps prevent lumps and ensures the batter is evenly mixed.

5

Add the sugar and stir until incorporated. The batter should be relatively thick and slightly grainy at this stage.

6

Pour the batter into the prepared pan and spread it evenly into the corners. For an extra chocolatey finish, sprinkle some chocolate chips over the top!

7

Bake for approximately 25 minutes. Check for doneness by inserting a toothpick into the centre. It should come out with a few moist crumbs attached, rather than wet batter.

8

Remove the brownies from the oven and let them cool in the pan for at least 30 minutes before lifting them out using the parchment paper. Slice into squares and enjoy!
