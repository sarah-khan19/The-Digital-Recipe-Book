Helpful tips!

Firstly, preheat your oven to 375°F and line 10 muffin cups in a standard 12-cup muffin tin with paper liners.

​

​

Then, beat in the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition to ensure they are fully incorporated into the butter and sugar mixture.

Next, using an electric mixer or a hand whisk, cream the butter until soft and smooth. Add both the white sugar and brown sugar, mixing until the mixture becomes light and fluffy. If whisking by hand, this may take a little extra time!



​

​

In a separate bowl, sift together the all-purpose flour and baking soda to remove any lumps and ensure the dry ingredients are evenly combined.

Gradually fold the dry ingredients into the butter mixture, alternating with the milk. Continue mixing gently until just combined, then fold in some of the chocolate chips, distributing them evenly throughout the batter.

Once your batter is ready, divide it evenly among the 10 prepared muffin cups. Sprinkle the remaining chocolate chips over the tops of each muffin for an extra chocolatey finish.

Lastly, place the muffin tin in your preheated oven and bake for at least 20 minutes, or until the tops are golden and a toothpick inserted into the centre comes out with a few moist crumbs. Allow the muffins to cool for at least 15 minutes before enjoying!
