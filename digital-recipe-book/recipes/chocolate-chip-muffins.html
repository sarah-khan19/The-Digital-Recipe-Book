Chocolate Chip Muffins

Bakery-style muffins that are soft, fluffy, and loaded with chocolate chips. Perfect with a cup of coffee!

Makes 10 muffins in a standard muffin tin
Prep Time 15 mins
Cook Time 20 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/3 cup melted butter
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Helpful tips!

  • For bakery-style muffins, line every other muffin cup to ensure plenty of room to rise without the muffins spreading into each other!

Instructions

1

Firstly, preheat your oven to 375°F and line 10 muffin cups in a standard 12-cup muffin tin with paper liners.

2

Next, using an electric mixer or a hand whisk, cream the butter until soft and smooth. Add both the white sugar and brown sugar, mixing until the mixture becomes light and fluffy. If whisking by hand, this may take a little extra time!

3

Beat in the egg, then stir in the milk.

4

In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt. Gradually add to the wet mixture until just combined.

5

Fold in the chocolate chips.

6

Spoon the batter into the prepared muffin cups, filling each about 3/4 full.

7

Bake for approximately 18–20 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the centre comes out clean. Cool briefly and enjoy!
