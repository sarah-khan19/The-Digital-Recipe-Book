Allow the funnel cake to fry until the bottom turns a light golden brown. Using tongs, carefully flip it over and cook the other side until it’s equally golden and crisp. Once fully cooked, remove the funnel cake from the oil and place it on a wire rack or a plate lined with paper towels to drain any excess oil.

Lastly, dust the warm funnel cake generously with powdered sugar. Add a scoop of vanilla ice cream on top, followed by a generous spoonful of your chilled strawberry compote. Serve immediately while the funnel cake is still warm and crispy for the best combination of textures and flavours.

Repeat the process with the remaining batter until you've made as many funnel cakes as desired!

Before you begin, make sure your strawberry compote is prepared and has cooled completely in the refrigerator. Having it ready ahead of time will make assembling your funnel cakes much easier.

Next, crack the eggs into a large mixing bowl and beat them lightly. Add the water, milk, and vanilla extract, whisking everything together until the ingredients are fully incorporated.

To start, whisk together the all-purpose flour, salt, baking soda, and sugar in a small mixing bowl until evenly combined. Set the bowl aside while you prepare the wet ingredients.



​

​

Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, whisking continuously to prevent lumps from forming. Continue mixing until you have a smooth, evenly combined batter.

Once your batter is ready, check its consistency. It should be thick but still pourable, slowly falling off a spatula. If the batter is too thick, gradually add a little more milk until it reaches the desired consistency. If it is too thin, add small amounts of flour until it thickens.

Now, prepare your frying oil. Pour enough oil into a deep frying pan to reach a depth of at least 1 1/2 inches. Heat the oil over medium-low heat, allowing it to warm gradually. While the oil heats, transfer your batter into a piping bag or a ziplock bag with a small corner cut off.

To check whether the oil is ready, carefully drop a small amount of batter into it. The batter should begin to sizzle and rise to the surface. If it browns too quickly, the oil is too hot, and the inside may remain undercooked. Keep the heat on the lower side and allow the funnel cakes to fry gradually so they cook evenly throughout.

Once the oil is ready, carefully pipe the batter into the pan in circular motions, overlapping the streams of batter to create the classic funnel cake pattern. Try to distribute the batter as evenly as possible so that the funnel cake cooks consistently.
