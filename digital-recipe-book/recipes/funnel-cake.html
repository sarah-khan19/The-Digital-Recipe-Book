Funnel Cake

Crispy on the outside, fluffy on the inside, and utterly addicting. Topped with powdered sugar and strawberry compote!

Makes around 6-7 funnel cakes
Prep Time 40 mins
Cook Time 10 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 3/4 tsp baking soda
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • strawberry compote
  • powdered sugar for dusting
  • oil for frying

Helpful tips!

  • Feel free to customize your funnel cake with any toppings you like! Fresh fruit, chocolate drizzle, flavoured ice cream, or even a sprinkle of cinnamon sugar are all delicious options.
  • If you don't plan to use all the batter on the same day, cover and refrigerate the remaining batter for up to 2 days until you're ready to make another batch!

Instructions

1

Before you begin, make sure your strawberry compote is prepared and has cooled completely in the refrigerator. Having it ready ahead of time will make assembling your funnel cakes much easier.

2

To start, whisk together the all-purpose flour, salt, baking soda, and sugar in a small mixing bowl until evenly combined. Set the bowl aside while you prepare the wet ingredients.

3

In a separate bowl, whisk together the egg, milk, and vanilla extract until smooth.

4

Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, whisking until a smooth batter forms. The batter should be pourable but not too thin.

5

Heat about 1–2 inches of oil in a deep skillet or pot over medium-high heat until it reaches 375°F.

6

Pour the batter into a funnel, squeeze bottle, or piping bag. Holding it over the hot oil, drizzle the batter in a circular, overlapping pattern to form a lacy cake.

7

Fry until golden brown on the bottom, then carefully flip and cook the other side until golden.

8

Remove the funnel cake with a slotted spoon or tongs and drain on paper towels.

9

Dust generously with powdered sugar while still warm.

10

Top with strawberry compote and any other toppings you like.

11

Serve immediately and enjoy!
