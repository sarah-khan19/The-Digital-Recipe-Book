Banana Crumble Muffins

These muffins are warm, cozy, and topped with the perfect buttery crumble. Soft banana flavour in every bite!

Makes 10 muffins in a standard muffin tin.
Prep Time 20 mins
Cook Time 20 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3 bananas
  • 3/4 cup white sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1/3 cup melted butter

Crumble Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1/8 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp butter

Helpful tips!

  • For bakery-style muffins, line every other muffin cup to ensure plenty of room to rise without the muffins spreading into each other!

Instructions

1

Firstly, line 10 muffin cups in a standard 12-cup muffin tin with paper liners. Preheat your oven to 375°F.

2

Next, in a medium mixing bowl, whisk together the all-purpose flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt until well combined.

3

Then, mash the bananas in a small bowl using a fork until smooth with small lumps.

4

In a large bowl, mix the mashed bananas, white sugar, egg, and melted butter until combined.

5

Gradually stir the dry ingredients into the banana mixture until just combined. Do not overmix.

6

For the crumble: in a small bowl, mix the brown sugar, flour, and cinnamon. Cut in the butter until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.

7

Spoon the batter into the prepared muffin cups, filling each about 3/4 full.

8

Sprinkle the crumble topping evenly over each muffin.

9

Bake for approximately 18–20 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the centre comes out clean.

10

Allow the muffins to cool in the tin for a few minutes before transferring to a wire rack. Enjoy!
