Helpful tips!

Firstly, line 10 muffin cups in a standard 12-cup muffin tin with paper liners. Preheat your oven to 375°F.



​

​

Then, mash the bananas in a small bowl using a fork until smooth with small lumps.

Next, in a medium mixing bowl, whisk together the all-purpose flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt until well combined.



​

​

In a separate mixing bowl, lightly beat the eggs. Add the melted butter, sugar, and mashed bananas, mixing well after each addition.

Once combined, gradually incorporate the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients, stirring until just combined and no large lumps remain. If needed, sift the dry ingredients beforehand to remove any clumps and ensure a smoother batter.

Now, for the crumble, combine the brown sugar, all-purpose flour, and ground cinnamon in a small bowl. Mix well using a fork.

Next, add the cold butter to the crumble mixture. Using a fork or your hands, work the butter into the dry ingredients until the mixture becomes crumbly, with small, soft clumps.

Now that your batter and crumble re ready, divide the batter evenly among the prepared muffin cups, filling each liner almost to the top.

Then, generously sprinkle the crumble topping over each muffin, making sure the tops are evenly covered.

Lastly, bake for 20 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the centre comes out with a few moist crumbs. Allow the muffins to cool in the tin for at least 15 minutes before enjoying!
